Teaching prompt: Move undergraduate enrollment from −3% to −5% to −10%. Notice total revenue falls only about half that percentage — why? (Net tuition is under half of all revenue, so the rest cushions the loss.) Then run the Demographic Cliff: the school raises discounting to 45% to hold the class, keeping more students but giving back more of every tuition dollar — and the deficit gets worse. Which lever actually protects the budget?

Illustrative model for classroom use. Net tuition = enrollment × sticker tuition × (1 − discount rate). Auxiliary revenue scales with housing occupancy; endowment distribution = corpus × payout rate. Expense budget held fixed at $500M so the operating margin isolates the revenue side.
